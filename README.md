## [MIB Neuralizer](neuralizer/): 3D-printable, light-up, no hardware

A parametric Men in Black neuralizer for a Bambu Lab P1S in PETG:
- **Sleeve:** the fluted sleeve slides open and a clear window under the dome glows red.
- **Button:** press HOURS for a white flash through the same window.
- **Hardware:** printed threads, snap-fits and a printed battery spring. No screws, springs, magnets or glue.
