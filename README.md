## [MIB Neuralizer](neuralizer/): 3D-printable, light-up, no hardware

A parametric OpenSCAD Men in Black neuralizer for a Bambu Lab P1S in PETG:
- **Sleeve:** a fluted sleeve slides open to reveal the button window and light the red ring.
- **Button:** pressing HOURS while it's open flashes the white dome.
- **Hardware:** printed threads, snap-fits and a printed battery spring. No screws, springs, magnets or glue.

- Build guide: [neuralizer/BUILD_GUIDE.md](neuralizer/BUILD_GUIDE.md)
- Proof report: [neuralizer/VERIFICATION.md](neuralizer/VERIFICATION.md)
- Model: [neuralizer/neuralizer.scad](neuralizer/neuralizer.scad), STLs in [neuralizer/stl](neuralizer/stl)
